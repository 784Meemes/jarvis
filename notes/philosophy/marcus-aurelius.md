# Marcus Aurelius

Marcus Aurelius was Roman emperor from 161 to 180 AD and one of the last of
the "Five Good Emperors." He is best known today not for his reign but for
Meditations, a private journal of Stoicism-flavored reflections he never
intended to publish.

The book reads less like philosophy-as-argument and more like philosophy-
as-practice: reminders to himself to stay disciplined, patient, and
unbothered by things outside his control. It's a useful counterpoint to
Epictetus, whose surviving work is lecture notes taken by a student rather
than a private diary.
