# Epictetus

Epictetus was born a slave, later freed, and became one of the most
influential teachers of Stoicism. His core idea, distilled in the
Enchiridion, is the "dichotomy of control": some things are up to us
(opinion, desire, aversion) and some things are not (body, property,
reputation) — peace of mind comes from investing your energy only in the
former.

This idea shows up, largely unattributed, throughout centuries of later
self-help writing. Marcus Aurelius studied Epictetus's teachings closely,
and Meditations quotes him directly in several places.
